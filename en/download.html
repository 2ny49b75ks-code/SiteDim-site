Frequently asked questions

Before you download

Is my device compatible?

If your iPhone or iPad has a LiDAR sensor and runs iOS 17 or later, it’s compatible with SITE DIM.

Does the app work offline?

Survey capture and calculations happen directly on the device. For specific technical questions, contact support.

What languages can I use it in?

SITE DIM is fully bilingual — French and English, switchable within the app.

How do I get help?

Email us at jdgeg@icloud.com or visit the Contact page.
